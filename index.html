Priority Distribution & Allocation Policy

Resource Allocation Strategy:

Disasters tagged as CRITICAL or HIGH receive priority dispatch of mobile relief units within 4 hours. The AI model heavily penalizes locations with high infrastructure damage scores and unaccessible road connectivity.

CRITICAL (Immediate Dispatch) 50% of active cases
HIGH (Dispatch within 12h) 33% of active cases
MEDIUM & LOW (Monitor) 17% of active cases
